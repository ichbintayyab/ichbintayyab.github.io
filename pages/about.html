My name is Muhammad Tayyab Saleem, and I'm a passionate Software Engineering student from Lahore, Pakistan. I'm currently pursuing my BS in Software Engineering from Lahore Garrison University, where I've successfully completed two semesters.

I have strong knowledge of Frontend Web Development and Python programming, and I continuously sharpen my skills through platforms like Udemy, Coursera, and YouTube. AI has played a major role in making my learning journey faster and more focused.

My long-term goal is to deeply understand Artificial Intelligence — how AI systems are designed, trained, and deployed — and eventually move towards the frontier of Artificial General Intelligence (AGI). I believe we're at the most exciting moment in human technological history.

Outside of coding, I enjoy learning new technologies, watching tech content, pencil sketching, and occasionally gaming. I believe in clean work, continuous learning, and professional ethics.
