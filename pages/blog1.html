My Journey Into Software Engineering

Muhammad Tayyab Saleem 2026 4 min read
✍️

Software engineering has become an important part of my life. My journey into this field has been both simple and fascinating — driven by curiosity for technology and a genuine passion for solving problems that matter.

Learning Phase

I am currently pursuing a BS in Software Engineering at Lahore Garrison University. Over the past two semesters, I've learned the fundamentals of programming, logic building, and how to work effectively within a team. These early experiences helped me understand how software development works in a real, practical setting — not just on paper.

University gave me structure. But the desire to learn faster pushed me beyond the classroom.

Online Learning

In addition to university classes, I explored platforms like Udemy, Coursera, and YouTube to accelerate my understanding. These resources allowed me to gain hands-on skills, work on small real-world projects, and see how programming concepts connect to actual applications people use.

What I found was that AI — tools like Claude, ChatGPT, and others — played a huge role in making my learning journey faster and more efficient. Being able to ask "why does this work?" and get a clear, contextual answer transformed how I approach new topics.

Projects & Practice

To solidify my knowledge, I started building small projects: calculators, to-do apps, basic websites, and a Python Tic-Tac-Toe game. These weren't impressive by any professional standard — but they taught me something that courses alone cannot: the experience of something actually working because you made it work.

Each bug fixed was a lesson. Each project completed was a confidence boost. I began to understand clean, maintainable code not as a rule, but as a form of respect for the person who reads your code next — including yourself, six months later.

Future Plan

My ultimate goal is to dive deep into Artificial Intelligence — to understand it not just as a user but as a builder. I want to understand how these systems learn, generalize, and eventually might achieve something approaching general intelligence. The concept of AGI (Artificial General Intelligence) genuinely excites me.

I believe we're at the most remarkable moment in computing history. Being a student right now — with the access, the tools, and the community we have — is an extraordinary privilege.

Conclusion

Software engineering has been an exciting journey so far — filled with learning, experimentation, frustration, and real growth. I look forward to continuing this path, expanding my skills, and one day contributing to something genuinely meaningful. The road is long, but every line of code makes it shorter.
